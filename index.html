Full Count

Do more with your GameChanger stats.

Full Count turns your player's GameChanger box scores into charts that show how they're improving, game by game and season by season. The games you add stay on your phone and in your own iCloud, for as long as you want them.

Coming soon to the App Store.

Many ways to import your data

Full Count takes a game in any of these forms.

A CSV or PDF file of the game

Share the file to Full Count. A CSV file has the most detail of anything you can add.

A CSV includes batting, pitching, fielding and catching.

Screenshots of the box score

Share the screenshots to Full Count. It adds the game for you and tells you if a number looks wrong.

Includes batting and pitching.

Just the final score, or no GameChanger at all

Type the game in yourself. Anything you enter shows up in your charts like any other game.

Includes whatever you type.

You can add fielding and catching to any game later. Open the game and tap Edit.

Every game, every season, in one place

Every game you add is saved on your phone and in your own iCloud. It stays there as your player changes teams, moves up an age group, and grows from one season to the next.

Full Count slices and dices your data to show you season and career totals and comparisons over time.

If your player guests for another club or plays on more than one team, it all adds up in one place.

Watch your player's progress

Stats for each game are charted with trend lines. Look at a whole career, one season, the last ten games, or any date range you pick. View progress game by game or season by season.

Charts also show what Good and Excellent look like for your player's age group, from 8U to 18U. When your player moves up an age group, the targets move with them.

Add games in batches

After a game, import the GameChanger CSV or PDF, or screenshot the GameChanger box score and share the pictures to Full Count. The game appears on your Home screen, in Games and in your charts. If a screenshotted number doesn't add up, Full Count points to it so you can fix it before you save.

After a tournament, send all your files separately or as a ZIP archive, from your phone, Google Drive or Dropbox folder, and import them in one set. Full Count flags numbers that don't add up, whether the screenshot is hard to read or the scorekeeper made a mistake.

The FAQ has every way to add games.

Add the whole team at once

Want to track every player? Choose Whole Team from the Import menu. Full Count lists all players in the lineups with checkboxes to pick which players you want to import.

Stats made for youth games

  • Runs Created from your own games. Runs Created with each way of reaching base worth what it actually produced in your player's games, compared with the other hitters in those games.
  • Per inning stats. Pro ball stats assume nine innings per game in baseball and seven in softball. Full Count lets you see stats per inning, or per game using the number of innings your league plays.
  • Targets for your player's age group. Measure progress against realistic and attainable goals.
  • Run support. How many runs the team scored for your pitcher.

Never forget a big game

Full Count spots the standout games for you: a home run, three hits, hitting for the cycle, a no-hitter, an errorless game, two runners thrown out stealing. Best Performances keeps them all on one screen and at your fingertips.

Share with the rest of the family

Share your player once, and everyone sees each new game you upload on their own phone. Those who just want to follow along get View Only access. Give Edit access to anyone who would upload games.

There are no new accounts to set up, and your player's stats stay in your own iCloud.

Set it up your way

Pick the stats and charts you want on your Home screen. Press and hold any chart to pin it to Home. Press and hold any stat to view the Glossary definition and how it's calculated.
